What this board is

The Player Board reads and Factor Confluence use the same shared seven-factor registry and thresholds. The production probability model uses the underlying prior-game role, scoring-environment, opponent-defense, and roster-continuity inputs; confluence is a consistent descriptive explanation of those inputs, not a separate ranking or automatic bet. Rule-era, rookie, injury, and expert-role layers remain monitoring-only.
