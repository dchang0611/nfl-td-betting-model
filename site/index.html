What this board is

The production ranking uses prior-game rushing and receiving role, red-zone and goal-line opportunities, team context, opponent defense, and roster continuity. Rule-era, rookie, injury, and expert-role layers are monitored separately and are promoted only after they improve an untouched test. Probabilities are estimates—not automatic bets.
